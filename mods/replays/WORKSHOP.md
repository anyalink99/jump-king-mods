[h1]Replays[/h1]

[b]Requires JK Runtime 1.30.[/b]

Record, watch and race your Jump King runs.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Recording saved on victory or manually from Pause
[*]Replay library in the main and pause menus
[*]Playback with play, pause and rewind controls
[*]A translucent racing ghost from any saved run
[*]Synchronized in-game timer and wind cycle, even for mid-run recordings
[/list]

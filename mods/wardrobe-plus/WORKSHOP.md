[h1]Wardrobe+[/h1]

Keep your favorite collection and give individual items a different look. Use a standalone boots reskin with a collection, borrow a hat from another set, or save your own combinations.

[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793086563]JK Runtime[/url] 1.30 or newer 1.x, and a compatible shared Harmony 2 engine.[/b]

[h2]Choose your look[/h2]
Open [b]Workshop > Wardrobe+[/b], or find Wardrobe+ in Mods settings. Choose a collection, select an item and pick its appearance. Every change takes effect immediately. Back navigates without discarding changes; Undo and Redo are available in More or with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Mix standalone reskins and individual collection entries.
[*]Equip and unequip owned items directly; preview the actual worn outfit.
[*]Apply Gold, Glass, Magenta or Cosmic to the body, individual clothing or the whole outfit.
[*]Move individual sprites into place, one pixel at a time, for all poses or a specific pose.
[*]Save complete outfits with equipment, skins, materials and positions; search, rename and share recipes.
[*]Type names with a physical keyboard or the shared Latin/Cyrillic gamepad keyboard.
[*]Favorite sources, randomize unlocked items and assign saved outfits to maps.
[*]Return to normal appearance while keeping your saved outfits.
[/list]

[h2]Controls[/h2]
Use the buttons shown at the bottom of the window. The first left click shows the cursor; click again to select, scroll to browse, and right-click to go back.

[h2]Materials[/h2]
Choose [b]Character or an item > Material[/b], or [b]More > Outfit material[/b] for the default across the outfit. Individual overrides take priority. Gold uses bright amber and lemon highlights; Magenta uses rich raspberry fabric shading; colorless Glass has translucent facets and bright edges, showing the map and lower clothing without distortion. Materials are included in saved outfits and recipes. Existing Diamond selections become Glass; Red velvet selections become Magenta.

Cosmic adds a crisp white outline around a black window into animated galaxies and stars. The star field stays anchored to the map as the character moves, with continuous screen seams, drifting layers and twinkling. Native and replay sprites animate; mods that flatten the appearance into a texture use a static cosmic version.

[h2]Positioning[/h2]
Select an item and choose [b]Move[/b]. Direction buttons move it one pixel; The character updates immediately. Done and Back keep the adjustment; Undo reverses a movement gesture. Pose-specific positioning is in the item's More options.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Wardrobe+ uses native equipment rules and item effects. It never grants unavailable equipment. Subscribe to the skins and collections you want to use; recipes contain references, not their artwork. Missing sources fall back without erasing your saved selection.

Use the updated Ball King build when combining its ball form with position adjustments. Clothing already drawn into a character's base texture cannot be removed automatically.

[h2]Your outfits[/h2]
Settings and outfits are stored in Jump King/Content/WardrobePlus, outside Workshop folders. [b]More > Restore normal appearance[/b] turns off Wardrobe's appearance overrides while retaining your saved looks.

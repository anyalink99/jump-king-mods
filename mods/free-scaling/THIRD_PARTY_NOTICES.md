Boundary-aware pixel sampling in JK Runtime follows the technique explained by
[Cole Cecil](https://colececil.dev/blog/2017/scaling-pixel-art-without-destroying-it/)
and [Claudio Santos](https://csantosbh.wordpress.com/2014/01/25/manual-texture-filtering-for-pixelated-games-in-webgl/).
The mod package contains no game artwork or game assemblies. The Workshop cover
uses Jump King's native king sprite, platforms and font, credited to Nexile.
Its renderer reads those assets from a local game installation.
